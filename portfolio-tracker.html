ⓘTwo pp methods, two different questions
Industry-level pp (shown on Spotlight cards and Industry Reference headers) is capital-weighted across positions — total $ deficit ÷ total invested. It answers: "how much pp alpha did the capital deployed in this whole industry produce?"
Per-position pp (shown in drill-downs and Strategy Reference rows) is just the position's actual deviation from its theme benchmark. It answers: "how is this single ticker performing vs its theme?"

Decision Scorecard
A structured pre-purchase checklist — it gives you the questions a disciplined buyer asks and organizes your answers, so every candidate runs the same gauntlet your existing positions are judged against. Field hints tell you where on Schwab to find each number and what a good/bad value looks like. After saving, each candidate gets checkable observations (computed facts, no recommendation) and an optional AI for/against/gaps analysis that argues both sides and never concludes — you weigh it. For ETFs it can fetch real holdings and flag overlap with names you hold directly. It does not score or recommend.
